About

Gilda Manfrin works with piano, sound, words and moving image. She studied piano privately and holds an MA in Sound Arts from London College of Communication, University of the Arts London. Her work has been presented at ADI Design Museum, Fondazione Sozzani, SPRINT and MilanOltre, through live performances, collective projects and commissioned work.
